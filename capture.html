New car or box set

This is where a car is added in the real app. It needs a phone camera and the PC, so here is the flow and what it produced for the sample box set.

  1. Photograph it. A carded car or loose car takes two photos; a box set takes six (front, back, top, bottom, left side, right side). The app crops and straightens each one.
  2. Research runs by itself. Google Lens identifies the car, the Hot Wheels Wiki gives the casting, year and series, sold eBay listings give the price and the wording sellers use, and the UPC is read from the box bottom.
  3. Review. The eBay-style page shows every field, with dropdowns that match eBay's own lists. Blank required fields are marked red.
  4. One click saves the row in the Garage and builds an eBay draft. You publish it yourself.
